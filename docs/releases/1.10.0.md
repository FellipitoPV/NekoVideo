# NekoVideo 1.10.0 - Private video recovery and faster browsing

### Features

- New and updated private videos carry recovery information so NekoVideo can recognize them after external moves or renames. Moving videos through the app remains recommended.
- Older private videos can be updated in the app after entering the password and confirming. Conversion includes subfolders, preserves paths and tags, and can resume after interruption.

### Improvements

- Faster private-folder shuffle, with tag filtering before playlist preparation and reuse of previously verified video information.
- Shuffle preparation shows its current stage and percentage in the progress dialog.
- Browse the last folder list while the startup scan runs in the background. Private folders open faster, and entering a folder or returning to the app checks for external additions silently.
- File operations reuse loaded lists without rechecking unchanged videos. Making folders private or removing protection updates the view as the operation completes.
- Tag backups follow each change, with smaller writes for video tag edits and retries after failed saves. Import checks integrity and preserves a recovery copy of the previous state.

### Fixes

- Automatic restoration of protected videos moved into ordinary folders respects private-folder visibility and does not make the destination folder private.
- Fixed the Remove protection action and repeated private-folder warnings without pending work.
- Progress dialogs follow the app theme and cannot be dismissed by dragging, tapping outside, or pressing Back.
- Removed the old custom secure-folder setting; password protection remains available for other folders.
