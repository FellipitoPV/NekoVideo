Fix

Ek samasya thik ki gayi hai jisme loading indicator seek, skip ya rewind ke dauran screen par bana reh sakta tha, bhale hi video normal tarike se chal raha ho.

Rare cases ke liye automatic recovery attempts bhi jode gaye hain, jahan koi video buffering me atak sakta tha.

Features

Ab player missing videos ko zyada clearly handle karta hai aur unhe dobara dhoondhne ke liye library refresh ka quick option deta hai.

Player se add ki gayi tags ab video grid par wapas aane par zyada consistently dikhai deti hain.
