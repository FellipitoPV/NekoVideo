Fix

Filtered folder selection now only considers items currently visible in the active filter.

Shuffle playlists are now limited to 1000 videos to avoid excessively large queues.

Features

Added a Sleep Timer with 5 to 90 minute options, a top indicator, a dialog to track remaining time, and an option to cancel the timer.

The video actions menu was reorganized into a side drawer, reducing the number of top buttons and improving screen space usage.

The screen now stays awake only during playback, and the Sleep Timer flow was refined to clear the current session and pause or resume the video correctly in related dialogs.

Sleep Timer drawer and dialog texts are now prepared for the app's supported languages.
